How to choose the right storefront sign for your building

[DATE] · [AUTHOR NAME] · 5 min read

Your storefront sign is often the first thing a new customer sees. The right one makes you easy to find and tells people what kind of business you are before they walk in. Here's what to think about before you order.

1. Who needs to see it, and from where?

A sign for drivers on a busy road needs bigger letters and fewer words than one for people walking by. Think about how far away your customers are and how fast they're moving.

2. Lit or non-lit?

If you're open after dark, or your street is busy in the evening, a lit sign keeps working at night. Channel letters and lightboxes are the most common lit options. Non-lit dimensional letters can look great for daytime businesses.

3. Check the rules first

Most exterior signs need a permit, and many plazas have their own sign rules for size, color and placement. Ask your landlord for the sign criteria before you design anything.

Tip: We handle permit drawings and approvals as part of our process, so you don't have to deal with the paperwork.

4. Keep the message simple

Your business name and what you do is usually enough. Save phone numbers, hours and specials for window graphics and other signs closer to the door.

5. Plan for Florida weather

Sun, salt air and storms are hard on signs. Ask about UV-resistant finishes, sealed electrical parts and proper mounting for wind.
