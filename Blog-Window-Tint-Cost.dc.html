1. Type of film

Standard dyed film is the most affordable. Ceramic film costs more but blocks much more heat without going darker, and it will not interfere with your phone or GPS. In Florida heat, most customers feel the difference right away.

2. Number of windows

Two front doors costs less than the whole car. Adding a windshield sun strip or clear heat-blocking windshield film adds to the price.

3. Vehicle size and glass shape

Trucks, SUVs and cars with curved rear glass take more time and film.

4. Removing old tint

If your old tint is purple, bubbling or peeling, it needs to come off first. Careful removal protects your rear defroster lines.

Tip: Florida has legal limits for how dark each window can be. We will show you the legal shades for your vehicle before we start.

Homes and businesses
